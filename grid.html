Scaling harness

Three bands · one block set · positions per band

Essay

Zoom, not reflow.

Within a band the composition is fixed. Column count, positions and spans do not change — only size. A screenshot at 1600px and one at 1400px are the same image at two magnifications.

Everything derives from a single scalar taken from the grid container, not the viewport. Gutters, cell size, type and spacing are multiples of it. Above the cap the grid stops growing and the margins absorb the surplus; that is the one deliberate break from pure zoom.
